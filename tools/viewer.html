Robo Chess is a 3D-printed robot arm that plays chess on a real board: NEMA 17 steppers driving ball screws and #25 roller chains, a printed 5.25:1 herringbone gearbox at the wrist and a servo claw. This page is its simulator, running in your browser; Stockfish picks the robot's moves.
