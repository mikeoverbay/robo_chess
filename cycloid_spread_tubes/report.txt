Assembly:
  1  Cut 6 tubes 42 long, ends square and deburred; press them up into the output shaft's holes till they bottom (13.5 stands out under its face).
  2  Motor to the housing floor: 4 x M3 x 8 from inside, heads into the counterbores. Housing ring on the floor.
  3  Steel pins: slide the 38 ring pins down the ring's D3.2 wall grooves into the floor's D3.4 sockets (tops stand 0.5 above the ring).
  4  No support ring: the tubes hang from the output shaft.
  5  On the bench, the eccentric cartridge: press a 6800 into each disc, grease the lobes. Disc 1's bearing up
     onto cam 1 FROM BELOW (over the neck) to the middle collar, then the eccentric spacer up the neck under it;
     disc 2's bearing down onto cam 2 from above, disc 2 turned 180 deg (the same part). Then press the top
     bearing (8 x 14 x 4, flange DOWN) onto the D8.025 journal on top, pushing on its inner ring only,
     until it is 0.2 above the D9 step (top of the journal flush with the bearing's top).
  6  Lower the cartridge onto the motor shaft, D to D, down to 0.3 above the floor, turning each disc on its
     bearing so its lobes fall between the ring pins, and grease its holes.
  7  Onto the flange journal: a 6808, the inner race spacer, the other 6808; push the lot up into the lid from
     below with the outer race spacer between the two outer races (the lip stops the top race);
     pin ring under the lid; lower the three together so the tubes go down through both discs' holes (turn
     the motor shaft till they line up) and the D14 pocket slides down over the top bearing (finger push) till the bearing's flange seats.
  8  6 lid screws through lid + pin ring + housing ring into the floor: they line the ring up on the floor and
     clamp the stack.  9  Clamp ring on the upper inner race, your disc
     on top, 3 x M3 into the flange inserts: this clamps both inner races to the flange.
  Fits to tune after a test print: --clear (backlash), --hub-bore, --cam-d, --ecc-bore, --pin-hole, --insert-hole.
