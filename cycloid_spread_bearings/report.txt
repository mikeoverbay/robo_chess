Assembly:
  1  Motor to the housing floor: 4 x M3 x 8 from inside, heads down in the counterbores.
     2  Housing ring on the floor.
  3  Steel pins: slide the 38 ring pins down the ring's D3.2 wall grooves into the floor's D3.4 sockets (tops stand 0.5 above the ring).
  4  On the bench, the output cartridge, built upside down on the flange (its underside up). Press a 6800 into
     each disc. Disc 2's bearing down onto cam 2 from the journal end, then press the top bearing (8 x 14 x 4)
     onto the D8.15 journal, flange toward cam 2, pushing on its inner ring only, till it is 0.2 above the D9 step.
     Lower the eccentric, journal down, so the top bearing goes into the flange's pocket: disc 2 lies over the
     flange face, its 5 holes over the flange's 5 bolt holes.
  5  Down each of disc 2's holes onto the flange face: a 0.5 spacer, 2 of the 3 x 6 x 2 bearings, a 2.5 spacer.
     Disc 1 (its own part) down over the neck onto cam 1, its holes over those stacks; down each hole 2 bearings and a 2.5 spacer.
     An M3 x 45 down through each stack into the flange (tight on the plain shank, then it cuts its own
     thread): snug, so the head pinches the stack on the inner rings. Check every outer ring still spins.
     The eccentric spacer onto the neck.
  6  Turn it over (the bolts hold everything). Lower it onto the motor shaft, D to D, turning the discs so
     their lobes fall between the ring pins; the bolt heads drop into the floor's groove.
  7  Both 6808s down onto the flange journal (a 6808, the inner race spacer, the other 6808, the outer race spacer between the outer races); pin ring onto the housing ring; the lid down
     over the 6808s (the lip stops the top race).
  8  6 lid screws through lid + pin ring + housing ring into the floor: they line the ring up on the floor and
     clamp the stack.  9  Clamp ring on the upper inner race, your disc
     on top, 3 x M3 into the flange inserts: this clamps both inner races to the flange.
