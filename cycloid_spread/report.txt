Assembly:
  1  Drill the printed pin holes to 3.0 (housing floor sockets, support ring, flange sockets) so the dowels press in.
  2  Motor to the housing: 4 x M3 x 8 from inside, heads into the counterbores.
  3  Steel pins: press the 38 ring pins down the wall grooves into the floor (tops stand 0.5 above the housing).
  4  Lower eccentric onto the motor shaft, D to D, down to 0.3 above the floor.
  5  Press the 8 output pins into the support ring (flush underneath) and drop it in over the hub.
  6  Press a 6800 into each disc, grease the lobes. Disc 1 down onto the lower cam and over the output pins;
     upper eccentric onto the shaft, its star socket down over the lower one's star (they lock 180 deg apart);
     disc 2 onto the upper cam, turned 180 deg
     (the same part; its lobes then fall between the pins).
  7  Onto the flange journal: a 6808, the inner race spacer, the other 6808; push the lot up into the lid from
     below with the outer race spacer between the two outer races (the lip stops the top race);
     pin ring under the lid; lower the three together so the flange sockets take the output pin tops.
  8  6 lid screws through lid + pin ring into the housing.  9  Clamp ring on the upper inner race, your disc
     on top, 3 x M3 into the flange inserts: this clamps both inner races to the flange.
  Fits to tune after a test print: --clear (backlash), --hub-bore, --cam-d, --ecc-bore, --pin-hole, --insert-hole.
